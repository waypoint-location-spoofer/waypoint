# Privacy and support

Pairing records and saved places are stored on your iPhone. Keep pairing records private because they authorize a developer connection to your device.

Map searches and map tiles use Apple services. Setup downloads developer support files. LocalDevVPN, Sideloadly, and idevice_pair are separate projects with their own documentation and privacy practices.

Read the [troubleshooting guide](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) first. For WayPoint problems, use this repository's Issues page. Do not send WayPoint support requests to the upstream StikDebug Discord.
